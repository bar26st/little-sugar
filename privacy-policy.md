# Privacy Policy — Little Sugar Spy

**Effective date:** May 1, 2026

---

## 1. Overview

Little Sugar Spy ("the App") is a mobile application that helps users track daily sugar intake for themselves and their family members by scanning food packaging with AI-powered image recognition.

This Privacy Policy explains what data the App processes, how it is used, and what rights you have.

---

## 2. Data Stored on Your Device

All profile and tracking data is stored **locally on your device only** and is never uploaded to our servers. This includes:

- User profile names, type (child/adult), age group, gender category, and daily sugar limit
- Food entries: product name, sugar amount, portion size, and date/time
- Daily sugar totals

This data can be deleted at any time by clearing the App's data in your device settings.

---

## 3. Data Transmitted to Third-Party Services

### 3.1 Food Photo Analysis (AI)

When you take or upload a photo of a food product, the image is sent to our backend service (powered by **Supabase**) for AI analysis. The purpose is to identify the product and extract nutritional information from the packaging.

- Photos are processed in real time and **not stored** after analysis is complete.
- Photos are not used to identify users or for any purpose other than food recognition.

### 3.2 Food Product Database

To supplement AI results, the App may query public food databases:

- **Open Food Facts** (openfoodfacts.org) — open-source product database
- **USDA FoodData Central** — US Department of Agriculture open database

These requests contain only the product name or barcode number — no personal information is transmitted.

---

## 4. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

- Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any personally identifiable information
- Device identifiers or advertising IDs
- Location data
- Contacts, camera roll, or files beyond the photo you explicitly choose to share

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App is designed for use by parents and families, including tracking sugar intake for children. The App does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. All data remains on the user's device and is controlled by the parent or guardian.

---

## 6. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

| Service | Purpose | Privacy Policy |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase | AI edge function backend | https://supabase.com/privacy |
| Open Food Facts | Product database | https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy |
| USDA FoodData | Product database | https://www.usda.gov/privacy-policy |

---

## 7. Data Security

- All data transmitted from the App to external services is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.
- No user data is stored on our servers.

---

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

- All data on your device can be deleted by clearing the App's storage in Android Settings → Apps → Little Sugar Spy → Storage → Clear Data.
- Photos sent for AI analysis are not retained after processing.

---

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the App and the effective date above will be updated.
